# nextCitizen — Product Context

## Product

**nextCitizen** is an Android application that helps users prepare for the U.S. citizenship civics test and naturalization interview.

## Company

**SanAkLabs**

GitHub account:

`sanaklabsinc`

Android package:

`com.sanaklabs.nextcitizen`

Google Play listing:

`https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sanaklabs.nextcitizen`

## Product Goal

Help applicants prepare with a clear, trustworthy, and easy-to-use study experience.

The product should reduce anxiety, support consistent practice, and help users understand civics content without making unsupported promises.

## Target Users

Primary users:

- People preparing for the U.S. citizenship civics test
- People preparing for the naturalization interview
- Permanent residents studying for naturalization
- Family members helping applicants prepare

Secondary users:

- Citizenship instructors
- Libraries
- Community organizations

Many users may be non-native English speakers. Public content should use plain, direct English.

## Verified Product Capabilities

The current product includes or supports:

- Civics question study
- Flashcards
- Mock tests
- Favorites
- Smart review / spaced review
- Progress-oriented study tools
- Local and state-specific answers
- Interview Mode marked **Coming Soon**

Do not present Interview Mode as currently available unless the product owner explicitly confirms release.

## Product Positioning

nextCitizen is positioned as a trustworthy study companion, not as an official government application.

Preferred positioning:

- Clear
- Calm
- Helpful
- Trustworthy
- Accessible
- Professional

Avoid:

- Guaranteed success
- “Best app”
- “Official app”
- Fake urgency
- Fake social proof
- Unsupported statistics

## Government Affiliation

nextCitizen is an independent educational product.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, or sponsored by USCIS or any U.S. government agency.

USCIS must be identified as the authoritative source for official immigration information.
